About

I am an associate professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Haifa. From 2018 to 2026 I was in the Department of Algebra at Charles University in Prague. Before that I was a postdoc at Ben-Gurion University, Bielefeld, Antwerp and the Weizmann Institute. I received my PhD from Ben-Gurion University in 2013 under the supervision of Amnon Yekutieli.

Research interests: commutative algebra, homological algebra, derived categories, commutative and noncommutative DG-rings, finitistic dimensions, and formalization of mathematics in Lean 4.
